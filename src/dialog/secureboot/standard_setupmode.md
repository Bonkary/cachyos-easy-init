## <div align="center">Setup Mode</div>
<div align="center"><strong><u>Important</u></strong>: We are <u><strong>NOT</strong></u> enabling Secure Boot yet. You won't be able to boot to CachyOS.</div>
<br>
I know people get scared in the BIOS, but I promise it's not <u>as</u> easy to break stuff as people make it seem as long as you use common sense. It's updating BIOS I worry about. Some of y'all be impatient on a slight hang. Anyways.

If you click <u>Reboot</u> (recommended), then it should reboot you directly into your BIOS. If you choose to reboot later, you can reopen this and the option will be there.
<br>

### Standard Motherboards
1. If your motherboard has an EZ Mode and Advanced Mode, you'll need to switch to <u>Advanced Mode</u>. Look around the screen and somewhere it'll tell you how.
2. There should be a tab called <u>Boot</u>. Go there.
3. Go to the <u>Secure Boot</u> section.
4. Select the <u>Reset to Setup Mode</u> option (or similar).
*Note: For an odd reason, some motherboards will ask you to if you want to 'Reset Without Saving', **please don't do that.***
5. After <u>Setup Mode</u> is enabled, go to <u>Save & Exit</u> and do just that.
6. Boot into CachyOS and reopen this app. I'll check if it worked.
